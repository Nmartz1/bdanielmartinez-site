About

B. Daniel Martinez is a former longtime Los Angeles radio personality (Danny Martinez) having worked at stations such as KIIS, K-EARTH, STAR 98.7, KFI, KRLA and KTWV (The Wave).

He also appeared in acting roles in film, television, and national commercials. Alongside his acting career he hit the road performing stand-up comedy in prestigious venues such as the Improv, Ice House, L.A. Cabaret, and Laugh Factory.

Following his radio career, he taught acting in a classroom setting and privately prepared working actors of all ages for auditions and for roles they acquired.

Today, B. Daniel Martinez is a prolific screenwriter, having written several screenplays. In addition to working on several new scripts, he has completed a contemporary single title romance novel, and is currently writing his first multi-cultural paranormal novel series.
